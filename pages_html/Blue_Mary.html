Gameplay Overview

[edit]

Blue Mary is one of, if not THE most aggressive character in the game. As a highly mobile grappler, Mary excels at fighting up close and personal, then pushing her advantage through nearly every single offensive weapon imaginable.

As a character who wants to move up close, Blue Mary has a few tricks up her sleeve to help her get in, most notably her Quick Step (236P/214P), a command dash in either direction that she can cancel into and out of, making her movement hard to predict. She can also high-profile or low-profile pokes with Spin Fall (236K) and Straight Slicer ([4]6K) respectively, both of which have knockdown follow-ups that highly reward Mary for slipping through an opponent’s neutral game.

Once Blue Mary has closed the gap, the fun really starts. Mary is a queen of close-range combat, sporting some of the greatest hitconfirms, plus frames, an overhead, multiple command grabs, the aforementioned cancelable command dash, and her unique Real Counter (214K) to dodge mashing and reversals and either punish with a command grab, or something stronger entirely. When she lands a hit, her combos deal extreme damage, are highly REV-efficient, and go into one of several hard knockdowns through her special move follow-ups; once Mary’s on top of an opponent, there’s no one better when it comes to mixing and pressuring an opponent until they crack. Not even jumping out is easy when Mary has a juggling air to air and two unblockable anti-airs in REV Vertical Arrow (623BD) and M. Dynamite Swing (236236K), making jump-outs and hops carry major risk.

It’s important to stress, however, that Blue Mary is all about the close-range, with very little to her name from afar. She has no good pokes for footsies, making her mid-range a hassle. Mary is thus a character who doesn’t play the patient game or lame anyone out — she gets up close and personal, and she lays down the hurt with some of CotW’s most oppressive offense.

Blue Mary is a highly mobile grappler who specializes in close-range combat, constantly mixing the opponent and enforcing guessing games.
Pros Cons
  • Close-Range Powerhouse: Blue Mary is nigh-unstoppable when she’s in the opponent’s face. With multiple command grabs, a spotdodge, great confirms, incredibly potent frame traps, a fantastic knockdown air to air, a command dash and backdash, a true anti air command grab, and the constant threat of strike-throw and high-low mix, Blue Mary can put opponents into horrible situations where it can feel like there’s no way out once she’s in, and capitalize on that better than almost any other.
  • Mobility: Blue Mary is incredibly nimble on the ground thanks to her special Quick Dashes, allowing her to dart into her ideal range, or out of an opponent’s. Her Spin FallWhich has a feint, and tricky timings. and Straight Slicer specials allow her to zip over or under an opponent’s attacks from MUCH further than her Dodge Attacks.
  • Hard Knockdowns: The hitgrab follow-ups on Blue Mary’s specials grant hard knockdowns which allow her to keep her offense going without having to worry about rolls.
  • Obliterating Life Bars At A Reasonable Price: Blue Mary’s link combos build up remarkably little REV for the sheer damage and okizeme that they provide, often clearing a lot of the built-up gauge through follow-up damage. This not only means Blue Mary can end rounds with shocking speed and efficiency, but it also affords Blue Mary more opportunities to spend REV elsewhere, such as in her mix-ups, frametraps, or anti-airs.
  • Closes Out Rounds Easily: Unique to Blue Mary are two anti-airs that can’t be Just Defended in REV Vertical Arrow and M. Dynamite Swing. This means you cannot simply turtle and attempt air JDs when at low health, as Mary can ignore that and kill you anyways.
  • Incredibly Rewarding Defense: Along with her efficient high damage, Blue Mary sports a command grab super that comes out in merely two frames, with the REV version being frame ONE. This means Mary can punish many being common pressure sequences, pokes, or enders that only Tizoc can, with a much faster motion. Additionally, Real Counter also grants her access to one of the most annoying just defend tools in the game.
  • Awkward Mid-range: Where Spin Fall, Quick Step (for projectile dodges), and Slicer grant her decent options from far away, they are incredibly risky up close. Mary's longest range pokes either have awful framedata or straight up whiff on crouchers as well. This limits her midrange capabilities, making it a defined danger zone for her.
  • Terrible High Dodge Attack: Mary's universal anti air has so little range that it will genuinely whiff against most jump angles unless the opponent puts out a button. Its height and angle aren't even that good, either. This makes it extremely risky to use in neutral, as empty jump whiff punishes are a realer threat than ever.
  • No Meterless Reversal: Blue Mary’s DP is not invincible to anything grounded, and Real Counter is no longer frame 1 invulnerable. As such, your only options are your supers, and don't be fooled - you CAN jump the command grab after the flash (unless it's REV).
  • Poor Light Confirms: Blue Mary's cancelable lights have an astonishingly low range, leaving most of her light confirms limited to her target comboThis means her potential reward off her command overhead is much lower than other characters with an one. However, having a command overhead is a major upside alone..

Mary grabs the opponent and gives them a back-breaking submission from behind.

  • Both versions have invincibility on startup.
  • Doing this super during Quick Step gives you a special version that combos properly on opponents in hitstun with no loss in damage (other than the scaling).
  • Opponents can jump the basic version after the super flash, but not the Redline version.

This grab super has fast startup, shockingly high damage, and multiple ways to land it.

Its very quick startup makes it great at punishing some moves that are otherwise safe, interrupting frame traps, and stopping guard point moves (like REV Blows), making it scary on defense. Unlike other grab supers, however, you can even combo into it; when you do this super from a Quick Step, you can grab opponents in hitstun, giving you a confirm with very little scaling. Since it’s only one hit, using it right before an opponent enters S.P.G will wipe out the vast majority of their health within it, and you can make them guess for the rest of it with the super’s great okizeme.

While the Redline version takes away the opponent’s ability to jump it on reaction, the minimal extra damage makes dumping both bars into it pointless. Just stick to the basic version in combos.

Mary jumps to knee the opponent, then giant swings them in the other direction.

  • Both versions have generous invincibility on startup.
  • Switches sides on hit.
  • Becomes an unblockable air grab if it doesn’t hit on the early active frames.
  • Taking into account her other command grab super - if used as a reversal, this forces her opponent into a quick reaction check to either block or jump in order to not get hit.

The Blue Mary classic. This super is your strongest combo ender when dumping REV into your special moves, usually as a cash-out or off of a successful mix-up via REV Accel.

It’s important to remember that it switches sides on hit. Since comboing into it will cause you to lose the corner, you have to change up your entire combo or just give up on the super entirely if you want to keep opponents cornered. On the flipside, that makes it extra scary as a reversal; if it works when you’re cornered, suddenly they’re the ones trapped and you can start your own offense.

Try not to use the Redline version as a reversal, as Mary’s supers are too strong to dump both bars just for the extra 90 damage. It’s fine to close out a round with it, as it’s your highest damage ender without Hidden Gear.
