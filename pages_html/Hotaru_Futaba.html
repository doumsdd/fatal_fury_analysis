Gameplay Overview

[edit]

Hotaru is perhaps the most well-rounded character in CotW. With all the basic tools a character could need and then some, she comes equipped for any situation thrown her way.

To properly underscore what Hotaru has, she has a fireball, a DP, a divekick, a launcher, a reflector, a command grab you can combo out of (if you REV it), several strong combo starters, and much more. She can rushdown, zone, pressure, grapple, play footsies, punish minute mistakes, mix up, cross up, turtle up, and do everything short of playing the game for you. If you can think it, Hotaru can do it.

Well-rounded as she is, though, she has a few quirks unique to her. She enjoys great mobility thanks to her amazing walk and run speeds, on top of having a divekick, letting her navigate neutral well and play offense or defense as needed. While she does have some minor shortcomings, like poor reward from lows and a reliance on REV Accel for her offense, there’s ways to play around these weaknesses, like command grabbing an opponent waiting out your offense, or playing a strong defensive game to reduce your reliance on offense.

If you’re wondering if this character is for you, the short answer is yes. She doesn’t rely too much on a defining gimmick or strength, but Hotaru being such a jack-of-all-trades is a boon in and of itself. To that end, Hotaru is one of the best characters for beginners, or players doing some soul searching for their next main.

Hotaru is an agile all-rounder, boasting a kit that is incredibly versatile. She is highly recommended for players new to CotW.
Pros Cons
  • Versatility: Hotaru has access to every tool you could ever want in the game, and she can execute her flexible gameplan anywhere on the screen. From far away, she can send out her slow moving fireball and follow behind it to force an approach, up close she has a command grab to mix people up, good frame data to apply pressure with blockstrings, and for defense she has the game's best 2A, fantastic reversals and a great Guard Cancel in her Rev command grab. Many of her tools are often exceptional in their own right and give her options in situations where other characters have none.
  • Air Options: Hotaru has a comboable air normal in j.C, a divekick and an incredibly spammable air Rev Blow that tends to beat even High Dodge anti airs, so she's both hard to meet midair or defend yourself against when grounded.
  • Fast: On top of her excellent fireball to cover approaches, Hotaru has the second fastest runspeed in the game, and her walkspeed is so good she walks backwards faster than some characters can walk forward, letting her do micro adjustments to weave in and out of ranges as she sees fit.
  • Reflector: Hotaru is one of only three characters who are able to send fireballs back at the opponent, giving her another tool with which to win fireball wars, approach to start her offense, or simply lame out the other player.
  • Low Damage: On top of her moves not hitting quite as hard as some other characters, nearly all of Hotaru’s bigger combos go through moves that add additional scaling, hurting her damage potential and causing her to require winning more interactions on average.
  • Vulnerable to Guard Cancels: Hotaru has limited options to pressure the opponent and all of them bring her up close, making her highly vulnerable to Hyper Defense and Guard Cancel DPs, command grabs, or Zero Frame supers, though this applies mostly to higher-level play.
