Gameplay Overview

[edit]

A series staple, Billy Kane’s gameplay in CotW is largely intact from earlier installments; he’s a bully character who is defined by his long reach coupled with surprising mobility.

Billy sports a unique moveset full of long-range pokes, closing the gap with his command normals, and high-damage knockdowns. When put together, it really lets Billy freestyle his offense and overall gameplan, giving him good neutral, pressure, and conversions, and letting him pick fights at ranges where the opponent effectively struggles to fight back. He even has no problem comboing into his Ignition Gears thanks to having solid confirms like a four-light sequence. Billy even hangs in tough with fireball users, as well; he can outtrade fireball users with his pokes, negate them entirely even when Overheated with his staff twirl (623P), or use his Redline Gear (236236D) to punish them outright.

All this control and offense comes at a price, though; Billy’s defense is downright terrible. With no meterless reversal or reliable anti-air beyond his Dodge Attack, Billy is very vulnerable to being pressured when knocked down, and it’s not uncommon to see one mistake from Billy turn into him getting mixed and pressured straight to a round loss. Not to mention, while Billy’s neutral is intimidating, it is not flawless, as his moves have long recovery on whiff and leave a large extended hurtbox for the opponent to hit with a Wild Punish.

With all that said, Billy is definitely a quirky character who is absolutely ruthless in the hands of a studied player. If wearing down your opponent until they crack is your style, consider sticking it to your enemies with Billy.

Billy is a mid range fighter who specializes in controlling space with his long-reaching staff normals and specials.
Pros Cons
  • Longest Pokes in the Game: Billy has the longest attacks, both normals and specials, in the game by a considerable amount, which often puts the opponent into situations where he can hit them while they can't effectively fight back.
  • Light Confirms: Billy's long range on his far lights means he can confirm consistently into 236A or a super, which puts significant threat behind his already strong foosties game.
  • Sandbox Mode: Billy's combo structure is extremely loose - his REV Art Senpuu Kon (623AC) has no juggle limit and he can use the D and REV Art version of his brakable Kyoushuu Hishou Kon (236K) in whichever order that pleases him. Score a good hit and the world is Billy's oyster.
  • Good Pressure: Aside from creating pressure using his superior range, Billy has access to a +3 c.B, +1 c.A, and even a +4 feint cancel in c.D, aided by his good dash. Despite his focus on ranged play, Billy is a menace on offense too.
  • No DP: Not having a meterless reversal or a brakeable DP puts Billy at a disadvantage against tighter pressure sequences and safejumps until he accumulates enough meter for a reversal super.
  • Slow Attacks: Sluggish frame data — especially on whiff — and a slower stepdash mean Billy must be careful on maneuvering the field and choose his attacks wisely, lest the opponent maneuver out of Billy’s preferred range, or worse, Wild Punish him off of his extended hurtboxes.
  • Resourceless Conversions: Billy’s REV moves do not naturally combo from light attacks, forcing him to spend a super bar to get a hard knockdown from these starters unless he’s close enough for Target Combo into REV Blow.
  • Weak mixups: Billy's mixup plan is centered around strike throw, as he doesn't have strong overheads,command throws,crossups, or set ups to open up the opponent.

Billy lifts his opponent up with his staff, then jumps after them and covers them in his staff's flames.

  • Has way better corner carry and range than his other supers, at the cost of damage.
  • Only the D version is invincible on startup.
  • If it hits an extended limb, the opponent will fall out as Billy goes through the whole animation, leaving him wide open for a punish on the ground. This never happens in combos.

A very long-reaching super that is quite combo friendly. It combos from all ranges of your lights, including the max range of far A.

B Version: Easily combos from light normals and various juggles, such as after 236BD and 214AC. Its superior corner carry combined with the range makes it ideal as your combo super midscreen, even if it doesn’t deal as much damage.
This is Billy’s best Zero Frame super by far unless you have Hidden Gear. Its range guarantees it reaches opponents no matter how they’ve spaced their move.
On block, only one hit connects against crouching opponents, but the super is extremely unsafe regardless of guard.

D Version: Reaches absurdly far (almost 2/3 screen) while becoming fully invincible on startup. This makes it a very strong projectile punish in addition to comboing from even more than the B version, such as single-hit confirming far C or some 214P juggle routes.
You can Brake this super, but you'll need to spend a lot of REV to make up the damage (although you get to build some meter back in doing so). The super Brakes automatically on block, but it’s still very unsafe.

Billy swings his extended staff like a baseball bat, triggering a cinematic on hit.

  • Resets the Rev meter to 0%, even during Overheat.
  • Only available during S.P.G. Damage value factors in S.P.G. bonus.
  • Huge range for a Hidden Gear and especially for a 6-frame move, making some things punishable exclusively by this move.
  • Very good okizeme compared to other characters’ Hidden Gears.
  • Like B/D super, you can confirm into this from lights, including max range 5A.

The highest-damage attack in Billy’s arsenal and also one of the fastest, this move is a powerful combo ender or punish that resets your REV gauge. Great for closing out rounds, using as a reversal, maximizing your damage, instantly escaping Overheat, or punishing some -6 attacks that are otherwise safe.
Worth noting is that unlike with other characters, Billy’s Hidden Gear actually has great okizeme rather than resetting to neutral. After the super, you can move back into Billy’s ideal range and potentially even meaty the opponent with an attack, such as 236P or his command normals.
Like Billy’s other supers, you can extend Hidden Gear’s range in juggles by cancelling a dash into it, input with 236Rev > 236Rev.
