Gameplay Overview

[edit]

Once an antagonist of Art of Fighting alongside Geese, Mr. Big has survived his former boss and arrived in CotW as an aggressive mid-range character whose playstyle could be described as a “poking grappler”.

Using his twin sticks, Mr. Big’s got lots of range on his normals, and his main objective in neutral is to use that range to wall out the opponent. Better yet, all of Mr. Big’s far normals cancel to special moves, like Ground Blaster (236P) to whittle away at the opponent’s health with chip, or his REV moves to turn those same pokes into big damage. For the peskier opponents who try to jump over Mr. Big’s normals, he has a vast assortment of anti-airs to knock them back down, like California Romance (623P), Silat Parry: Alpha (214A) and a great High Dodge Attack.

Once a poke converts to a knockdown, that’s when Mr. Big shows his true strength: he’s a relentless offensive powerhouse. He’s got it all in that department: overheads, great lows, plus-frame normals, light-to-REV conversions, command grabs, tricky Brakes, the works, and it can all lead to looping okizeme. They work in tandem with his poking game to keep the opponent locked down, forcing them to weather Mr. Big’s attack, with Silat Parry in the back pocket to deal with opponents trying to fight their way out. Suffice to say, once Mr. Big is in, the opponent is in BIG trouble.

Although Mr. Big does have a notable weakness to zoning, and tends to struggle on defense to his DP lacking true invincibility, all it takes is one opening — one wrong move against his poking game — to give Mr. Big the chance to devastate an opponent’s life bar.

Mr. Big is an aggressive poking character who harasses his way in before assaulting his opponent with high-damage mix-ups.
Pros Cons
  • Fantastic Poking: Mr. Big’s poking/mid-range game is among the best in the business. All of his far normals are special-cancellable, and are backed up by safe specials like his projectile, Crazy Drum, and Brakable specials.
  • Mix-up Monster: Mr. Big possesses an oppressive high-low game with a nasty overhead. To top it off, he even has a command grab with excellent oki, particularly in the corner. Landing this command grab immediately forces the opponent into yet another high/low/throw mix. If that's not enough, Mr. Big can even threaten a 1-frame super command grab that cannot be jumped post-flash, even from beyond its usual range with a dash cancel or Brake.
  • BIG Damage: Mr. Big's best combos all deal above-average damage even without a super tacked on the end, and they're all quite easy to get started. He also boasts some of the best meterless damage in the game, making him a threat even in Overheat. With resources and a Counter Hit, Mr. Big can net upwards of 700 damage!
  • Anti-Air Assortment: On top of harassing opponents on the ground, Mr. Big controls the air with a wide range of anti-airs, including brakable DPs, a fast high parry with a manual follow-up, great high-hitting normals, and even an anti-safejump move in his command grab super.
  • No Meterless Reversal: Although Mr. Big has a DP, his is not truly invincible, and he is thus forced to rely on his supers for invincible wakeup.
  • Zoned Out: Even with a REV projectile, Mr. Big lacks a reliable way to circumvent zoning. He is very weak from beyond poking range and needs to be patient and make good reads to get in on characters that have long range harassment tools.
  • Block Advantage: Outside of his close D which can be Hyper Defended, Mr. Big’s normals on average have worse advantage on block than other characters. If he doesn’t commit to a special cancel, opponents have larger windows to mash out and take their turn back.

  • Mr Big's fastest anti-air, and it hits twice
  • Hits from slightly shorter than round-start distance, or the distance of a full jump

  • Adds a long dash before attacking. Despite retaining air-invulnerability, this is way too slow to be used as an anti-air, and so it is mostly used as combo filler.

    • If braked during the startup dash on the ground, immediately enters recovery frames after 9 frames of air-invulnerable dashing
    • Otherwise, can be braked right as the attack becomes active to stop Mr Big from going airborne and cancel the third hit. This is still barely punishable on block, unlike other braked anti-air specials.

Rev version (623AC):

  • Slightly slower than the light version, but with longer range. It is still fast enough to combo from any special-cancelable attack, making it an excellent tool for starting combos from lights.
  • Has the same two brake points as the heavy version, but is actually safe on block
  • Braked version launches the opponent higher than the braked heavy version
