Gameplay Overview

[edit]

Kevin's game is not a hard one to grasp. He's loaded with big hitboxes, big damage and a character-defining super attack that turns every poke into a serious threat with a bit of practice on single-hit confirms.

Kevin comes with two primary objectives: First, hold midrange spacing with his beefy hitboxes. Second, close the gap on the opponent and take advantage of his powerful close-range mixup tools. In both cases, Kevin will be looking to cash out for big damage. Approaching the opponent will be difficult, but approaching Kevin will be equally difficult in return. The strengths that define Kevin are also his weaknesses: An opponent that is too passive will give Kevin a free pass to access his dangerous mix, while an impatient player will be blown up all the same for running into any of Kevin's pokes. All the same, approaching the opponent will be a test of patience balanced with a test of the player's ability to stay active on defense.

Play this character if you enjoy access to big hitboxes and can accept a patient flow of gameplay that requires surgical timing and decision-making. Single-hit confirming and whiff punishing are the most important skills to develop to capitalize on Kevin's stellar damage output, and it will be generously rewarded. Avoid this character if you want something a little more high-octane or struggle to make use of Fatal Fury's more advanced defensive techniques, such as guard canceling.

As a final note, hit-confirming normals into Gatling Freezer (236236B) is an absolutely critical skill for playing Kevin. This is a character-defining technique that heavily influence whether or not you enjoy playing the character.

Kevin is a strike-heavy brawler who brings heavy-duty firepower to the fight, rewarding patience and good hit-confirms with explosive returns on investment.
Pros Cons
  • Big hitboxes: Kevin comes loaded with huge hitboxes on nearly all of his attacks, allowing him to easily control space and threaten deadly whiff punishes.
  • BINGO!: Kevin is able to achieve explosive returns on any hit thanks to a massive Close C activation range and the ability to hit-confirm any far normal into Gatling Freezer (236236K)
  • Close-Range Mixups: Once Kevin has closed the gap on his opponent, he gains access to a lethal arsenal of high-damage mixups that include left/right mix with EX Roll (214BD), a command grab, a crawl that comes pre-packaged with high/low mix, and frametraps that complement his ability to shred Rev Meters better than most (possibly all) other characters in the game.
  • Difficulty getting in: Kevin can struggle to close gaps between him and his opponent. His slow speed and lack of access to any reliable non-standard movement can make it difficult to keep up with a retreating opponent, and he sometimes struggles with getting his close-range mix started.
  • Volatile Defense: Kevin lacks any sort of reversal outside of his Lucky Strike (236236P), Redline Gatling Freezer (236236D) or Hidden Gear super attacks, which require at least one full stock to use and are still not good against jumping opponents. This makes Kevin dependent on the game's advanced defensive techniques (Rev Guard, Guard Cancels), with the silver lining that Gatling Freezer (236236B) is an especially rewarding Zero Frame Super and landing a hit with REV Blow grants him access to deadly mixups on the hard knockdown.

Universal target combo that can be accessed on hit or block, starting by chaining Close A into C. Sometimes abbreviated as ACCD.

Close A > C: Can be canceled into the third hit of the target combo, or can be skipped to go straight to a finisher or REV Blow.

Close A > C > C: Third hit that can only be canceled into a finisher or REV Blow.


This target combo has 3 finishers, each activated by pressing D while holding a direction:

5D: Soft knockdown on hit, uses the same animation as Far D. Not a mixup and unsafe on block if not canceled. The only finisher that can be canceled into REV Blow

6D: Overhead finisher. Uses the same animation as 5CR, except now it is +2 on hit or block. Can be low-profiled by 2CR or interrupted with a sufficiently fast attack.

2D: Low finisher. Identical to using 2D in any other situation, and is just as unsafe on block.
