Gameplay Overview

[edit]

Robert Garcia, hero of old from the Art of Fighting series, dons the tengu mask to become Mr. Karate, a highly technical, defensive character who combines the explosive combos of Kyokugen karate with ironclad defense, turning opponents’ aggression against them.

It’s difficult to cover everything Mr. Karate can do, since he can do so much. But his basic gameplan is to play the neutral game, shutting down opponents’ long range with his stance and fireballs, playing footsies with his quick far D, punishing from a distance, and even a unique taunt that eats away at patient foes’ REV. This gradually goads the opponent into coming closer, where they are forced to then contest with Mr. Karate’s stout defense via fast buttons, invincible DP, parry from stance (which itself makes an incredible Guard Cancel option), and incredible anti-airs. Mr. Karate can then turn this defense into quite high damage thanks to brakes, REV Accel, his unique Dragon Burst cancels, and stance transitions.

It seems at this point like Mr. Karate can do everything, but he does have a major weakness: a very risky offense. He lacks plus frames of any kind from beyond throw range, leaving his offense limited to highly committal, read-based mixups. He also has to be careful not to use too much REV, as he stands to lose much more in Overheat, including many of his unique mechanics. Thankfully, he has some incredible resourceless combos and a Spirit Charge to keep his REV down for when he needs it in his combos.

While having weak offense contrasts a good majority of the roster, that hardly matters when Mr. Karate’s myriad of defensive weapons can stop offense on the spot. Thanks to these defensive tools, this character’s skill ceiling and tech depth are unbelievably high, making him a great character for passive player and lab monster alike.

Mr. Karate is a technical all-rounder with stylish combos, defensive prowess, and ability to manipulate both his and his opponent's REV gauge.
Pros Cons
  • Long-Range Menace: Mr. Karate is uniquely strong at fighting from a distance. He completely shuts down other characters’ projectiles, either with his own fireballs or with Drago Supremo’s built-in reflector, and can harass from well beyond their range with Hien Shippuu Kyaku. If the opponent isn’t convinced to approach by this, Mr. Karate can punish their patience by Razzing them, driving up their REV gauge without them doing anything.
  • Art of Anti-Airing: Mr. Karate has every anti-air you could ask for and then some. On top of having a great DP that he can combo off of, he has a solid 2C, great normals for air-to-airing, and even a juggling anti-air normal in far D + its Command Combo for distant anti-airing.
  • Crazy Combo Potential: Mr. Karate can deal great damage and corner carry on nearly every hit thanks to Dragon Burst cancels, Brakable anti-airs, multiple multi-hit normals that make confirming a breeze, and ability to combo one of his Ignition Gears into the other in the corner. With Mr. Karate, you can make every hit really hurt if you’ve got the skill.
  • Drago Supremo: Mr. Karate’s stance can do almost anything, on offense or defense. He can make numerous offensive or defensive plays with its many followups, Guard Cancel into it to shut down offense, reflect fireballs, reset into it to push strike/throw, incoporate its followups into combos, bait whiffs, and so much more. The possibilities with it are almost endless.
  • Great Defense: On top of his solid anti-airs and invincible DP, Mr. Karate has some of the best Guard Cancel options in the game, between his heavy DP counter hit launching, counterattacks from Drago Supremo, and Dragon Step to dodge attacks that he would otherwise be forced to block. When cornered, he gains a wall jump to escape the corner.
  • 👺
  • Stance-Locked Mix-Ups: Mr. Karate’s most potent mix-up options are all locked behind his Drago Supremo stance, making many options to open opponents up quite committal unless he finds a hard knockdown that gives him access to his Mule Kick.
  • Very Slow Fireball: Although Mr. Karate's fireball beats others’ fireballs clean, it is by far the slowest to start up, and as such can easily be jumped and punished on reaction if the opponent is in range to do so. The animation of his feint is also too distinct from the real fireball to fool any seasoned player.
  • Loses Even More in Overheat: On top of his DP losing invincibility, being Overheated costs Mr. Karate his ability to charge his fireball and DP, and his fireball and Hien Shippuu Kyaku lose their ability to cause knockdowns, costing him a ton of meterless damage potential. However, he can still use Spirit Charge to Cooldown faster.

A decent long-ranged kick with largely the same range as far C, except that it is 2 frames faster and can’t be cancelled to every special. It has a deceptive disjointed hitbox that does not miss crouchers even at a distance. However, several quirks exist with this move that no other normals in the game possess.

  • It can be selectively performed even at close distances by pressing 5B+D

Additionally Drago Supremo cannot counter attacks that are behind Mr. Karate such as cross-ups. When a parryable strike connects, Mr. Karate will automatically follow up with Dragon Roar. Even if the opponent was airborne when they triggered Dragon Howl, they will be forced into a grounded state for the animation. REV Dragon Howl can be followed up with Dragon Burst or a REV Accel. This makes Drago Supremo a good option when attempting a Guard Cancel, as certain REV Accel blockstrings that blow up other Guard Cancel options like a REV Accel into a REV DP will be countered by Dragon Howl.
