Gameplay Overview

[edit]

Mai in CotW is one of the more defensive characters in the game, being well-rounded with emphasis on neutral control and mobility.

Mai can command the neutral with her great moveset that consists of a projectile, brakable anti-air, and some of the game’s best neutral pokes. These alone do well enough, but as icing on the cake, Mai retains all of her mobility options from older games, such as her wall jump, making her better still at playing the keepaway game. For when Mai lands a hit and has the advantage, she has multiple ways to keep her turn, including multiple plus-on-block special moves and ways to reposition at will.

Mai's weaknesses come in the form of inconsistency.; while she has lots of plus frames, she doesn't have any great mixup options, and her multihit specials are weak to Hyper Defense. Her normals are great, but she can struggle to connect some of them against crouching opponents in neutral, her fan toss is uniquely strong in exchange for not having an anti-fireball super, and her damage is lower than other characters until she gets a super stocked.

If a highly neutral-oriented take on a CotW shoto with some stylish optimal combos sounds like fun, consider trying Mai out.

Mai Shiranui is a neutral-focused character with good normals, mobility and zoning, with little emphasis on offense.
Pros Cons
  • Amazing Footsies: Mai has great range on all of her normals, especially her light attacks which are potentially the best in the game. While 3D lets her safely fish for counterhits from midrange and serves as a long range, safe low attack which are rare properties for slide attacks to have. Combined with her great walkspeed and massive range buttons which have great conversion ability. Mai can play a very effective footsies game. There is no real area or ranges where Mai is weak which makes her quite stable to play into various matchups.
  • Zoning: Mai is equipped with a DP and a great fireball, letting her play a traditional shoto fireball game, supplemented by an occasional airthrow. Mai also has best in class air to airs in j.A and j.C . Additionally, her EX fireball excels at countering other EX fireballs specifically, and it can let her rapidly approach in cojunction with her command jump. Her walljump and backwards command jump also give her better runaway than most characters.
  • Mobility: Mai has access to a variety of advancing attacks, a command jump, and also excels in the air with tools like her divekick, wall jump, and j.2B to stall momentum, letting her mix up her approach and stay unpredictable.
  • Just hold downback bro...: Mai's offense is as basic as it gets, and she doesn't have any majorly powerful tools to make it that threatening, lacking good high/low mixup tools in particular. This invades other aspects of her gameplay too, as her only consistently rewarding heavy poke whiffs on crouchers at range, which hurts her risk reward significantly in neutral and pressure.On top of her plus on block REV special moves having significant pushback on block. Opening up opponents with Mai takes considerable effort.
  • Low Average Damage: Mai tends to deal below average damage on hits when she doesn't have a super available, and all of her grounded normals lack extra reward on counterhit. Note that her super/corner combos are very powerful, however the best ones are also VERY difficult.
  • Weakness to Hyper Defend: Every Rev enhanced special Mai has is multi-hitting with a long animation time (the ones viable in pressure at least), making them easy to counter for experienced players. Both of her close heavies are also multi-hitting.
  • Significant pushback on plus on block moves:All of Mai's moves except for close D push her back so far that she is out of throw range. Mai has a hard time setting up throws except by taking significant risks like dash up throwing or walking up slowly and throwing. Against overly defensive opponents, Mai will have to take alot of risks to land a throw or a 2B. She is also unable to tick throw after a blocked cl.A. Even with Mai's amazing frame traps, if the opponent is willing to take block your frame trap attempts, there is not much Mai can threaten with.
